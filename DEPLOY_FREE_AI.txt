MANAGER-AI — FREE ONLINE AI AUDIT

Current AI route:
Manager.io -> Manager-AI -> Vercel -> OpenRouter -> openrouter/free -> available free model

Vercel Environment Variables:
OPENROUTER_API_KEY = your OpenRouter API key
OPENROUTER_MODEL = openrouter/free

Important:
- Keep OPENROUTER_API_KEY server-side in Vercel only.
- Set OPENROUTER_MODEL to openrouter/free. This prevents the project from depending on a specific free model that may become unavailable.
- The endpoint uses structured JSON output and medium reasoning effort to reduce timeout risk.
- Vercel function maxDuration is 60 seconds. The server aborts an AI call after 50 seconds and returns a clear error instead of hanging.
- The compact Manager transaction page does NOT expose a running-balance column. Its blue bottom amount is treated as a transaction total, not as the account ending balance. The audit therefore does not invent a balance comparison when a real running balance is unavailable.
- Inter Account Transfer is treated as a review signal, not automatically as a high-severity accounting error. The AI receives the full ledger and deterministic signals and must validate the context.

After changing OPENROUTER_MODEL in Vercel, Redeploy Production.
